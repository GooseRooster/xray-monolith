## Summary
30 commits. Two distinct clusters: (1) a large, well-tested incremental series
by TheLostInPlace/dami.sirbu (2026-06-03 through 2026-07-15) adding ~25 new
Lua AI hooks/exports and cvars for combat tuning (cover selection, aim params,

disguised as a "fix" commit and deserves a playtest pass before release, and
one real crash bug (`0243d6b8`) is worth a follow-up fix.

## Hot-zone / deep-read commits

### 0243d6b8 - CP (stationary mgun visibility override)
Adds `WeaponStatMgun::OverrideRangeFOV` (Lua-scriptable visibility-range
override for stationary machine guns, matching triage's read - gated by
`#ifdef STATIONARYMGUN_NEW`/`HOLDERCUSTOM_NEW`) and two new script exports,
`GetObjectVisibleDistance`/`GetObjectLuminocity`, on `CScriptGameObject`.
**Bug found**: both new exports log an error when their null-check fails
(`obj == nullptr` / `custom_monster == nullptr`) but do not `return` after
logging - they fall through and dereference the null pointer anyway. Real
crash bug in the upstream diff, not a divergence issue → `bug_found`. Not a
reason to skip (still additive/opt-in), but flag for a follow-up one-line fix
(add the missing `return`s) before/after landing.
Verdict: unchanged (take).

### e861de7d - AI Configuration & Fixes
Confirmed hot-zone hits (`Weapon.cpp`, `console_commands.cpp`) are trivial as
triage found, but the full diff is a substantially bigger behavior change than
the "AI Fixes" framing suggests:
- Enemy-target threat scoring collapsed from -1500 (actor)/-500 (other) to
  -5 (<30m)/-100 (else), and the `victory_probability` weighting term is
  removed entirely. This is a fundamental rework of how stalkers pick which
  enemy to engage, not a bugfix.
- `CWeapon::CheckForMisfire` is now hard-disabled for every non-actor holder
  (all NPC weapons never misfire from engine logic again, regardless of
  weapon condition).
- New teammate-stacking fallback and squad-spacing cover penalty (behavior
  addition, not present before at all).
- `ai_move_to_cover_run` lets NPCs sprint to cover/lookout instead of walking.
- **Undocumented side effect**: `CDangerManager::add()`'s injuring-sound path
  used to skip adding a danger event when the sound's originating actor
  wasn't an enemy-relation to the perceiving NPC; that relation gate is
  removed here with no mention in the commit message. Net effect: NPCs now
  register danger from *any* actor's injuring sounds regardless of relation,
  which the commit message doesn't call out at all among its documented
  changes.
All of the above are gameplay/feel changes a player would notice → `playtest`.
Verdict: unchanged (take) - it's in-scope AI tuning with no OWA divergence
overlap, but flag for a combat playtest pass, ideally isolating the
danger-relation-gate removal specifically since it's not in the stated scope
of the commit.

### 7b1428e0 - Burer and poltergeist telekinesis fix
Verified triage's read directly: adds `gcontact_Initiator` to
`CPHMovementControl` so collision-damage attribution can be redirected to the
telekinesis caster instead of always defaulting to
`ICollisionDamageInfo::DamageInitiator()`. `ICollisionHitCallback::call`'s
signature changes from `IPhysicsShellHolder*` to `CObject*&` (settable, so a
callback can null out/redirect the initiator) - checked both implementers
(`burer_state_attack_tele.h`, `poltergeist_telekinesis.cpp`) and both were
updated consistently in the same commit, plus a real memory leak fix
(`~CCharacterPhysicsSupport` now `xr_delete`s the callback instead of just
detaching it). No gotchas found; confirms triage's "no overlap with
FPCam/movement-inertia customizations" read.
Verdict: unchanged (take).

## Gotchas / follow-ups

- **`bug_found`** (`0243d6b8`): `GetObjectVisibleDistance`/`GetObjectLuminocity`
  in `script_game_object3.cpp` dereference a possibly-null pointer after
  logging (but not returning on) the null-check failure. Real crash risk if
  a script ever calls these with a bad object. Worth a follow-up one-liner
  fix, not a reason to skip.
- **`playtest`** (`e861de7d`): combat-AI targeting/misfire/squad-behavior
  rebalance bundled into a commit titled "AI Configuration & Fixes" -
  recommend an actual firefight playtest before this lands, specifically
  checking (a) whether NPCs now feel too tanky/aggressive at close range
  given the flattened hit-penalty curve, and (b) whether the removed
  actor-relation gate on injuring-sound danger perception causes any new
  false-alarm behavior in allied-NPC scenes.
- **`playtest`** (`e4de4f5c` + `23466440`): net effect roughly halves the
  stalker anomaly-bolt-detection inertia window (15000+rand(5000)ms →
  5000+rand(3000)ms) and adds a 60s suppression cooldown - NPCs will cycle
  through bolt-throwing at anomalies noticeably faster/differently than
  before.
- **`ordering_after`** (`703a0cc7` → after `15596c8e`): see above; already
  satisfied by chronological take-order, flagged for apply's determinism.
- **Apply-time note (not a structured flag)**: `e861de7d` and the
  `67d15d3b`...`3594e030` series both touch `console_commands.cpp` and
  `stalker_combat_actions.cpp` densely (different variables, no logical
  conflict found), but cherry-picking will produce adjacent/interleaved
  hunks in those two files - expect textual (not semantic) merge friction
  during apply.
